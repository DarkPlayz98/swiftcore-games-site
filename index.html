Edge Of Honors

BetaPrivate, being reworked

Fight your friends with swords and earn money to buy better ones. Whatever sword you have equipped comes with you into every server.

Swords right now: Basic, Fire, StormCutter, Golden, Sand, Bone and Block. The Golden Arena is one of the maps.

We're redoing all the systems to meet Roblox's newer standards. New maps stay, older ones get reworked, the swords get remodelled and a server-side sword system is coming.

20 players, R6, private since 25 May 2026. 2,040 visits, 127 favourites, 37 up and 1 down. First published 17 Feb 2025, last updated 21 Nov 2026.
